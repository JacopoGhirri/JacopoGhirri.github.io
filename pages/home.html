Jacopo Ghirri

ML/AI Researcher and R&D Engineer

I am an R&D engineer and AI researcher with a PhD in mathematical modeling, deep learning and statistical inference from Politecnico di Milano. I build machine learning models (generative models, deep time-series forecasting, variational inference) for large-scale data, with a focus on uncertainty quantification and robust decision-making.

I currently work in ML/AI research and development at Kline in Milan, applying machine learning to risk management, forecasting and optimization in the financial sector. Previously, I was a visiting researcher at Mila – Quebec AI Institute, and an affiliated researcher at the RFF-CMCC European Institute on Economics and the Environment.
