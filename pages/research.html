Research

Short term CO2 emission forecasts in the EU

Traditional forecasting methods struggle with many highly correlated variables. I developed a deep learning pipeline for short-term CO2 emission prediction across 27 EU countries and multiple sectors, handling many emission drivers and transition indicators.
It makes it possible to assess how traditionally computed scenarios compare with data-driven trend extrapolations.

A new generation of Climate Scenarios

Integrated Assessment Models are pivotal in shaping climate policy, but are limited by their optimization framework.
At Mila I adapted GFlowNets, a generative model architecture, to explore high-dimensional policy spaces in macroeconomic climate simulators, yielding a more diverse set of feasible mitigation strategies and assessing the robustness of optimal solutions.

Robust decision-making under model uncertainty

A decision-making framework based on multiplier preferences and statistical divergence measures, complemented by function-on-scalar regression emulators of integrated assessment models with Gaussian-process residual sampling and Monte Carlo propagation of climate and damage uncertainty.
